## 投影片 2

# Bjective of Wine Tasting（= Degustation）品酒的目的
1.  To know the characteristics of wine.  /  了解酒的特性
2.  To judge the quality and appropriate price of wine.
     判斷葡萄酒的質量和合適的價格
3.  To judge the maturation period and storage period.
     判斷葡萄酒的成熟期和貯藏期
4.  To phrase the wine characters.  / 為葡萄酒下註解
To assume matching food and service methods to
　  enhance the wine characters.
　  進行對葡萄酒的結構與風味了解，進而與食物的配對與正確的
　  服務方式（溫度、過瓶等）

## 投影片 3

As these are the objectives of professional tasting, tasters should not have any bias or pursue personal preferences while keeping positive attitudes.
由於這些都是專業品酒的目標，因此侍酒師不應該有任何的偏見或追求個人的喜好，同時需保持正面的態度給予評價和服務

Tasting of a wine is to know the past (the wine was made where, from which grape and how), the present (current condition, how to enjoy) and the future (how it will develop, how long it can be preserved) of each wine.
品嚐酒的目的是：要了解分析酒的過去（其葡萄品種、生長條件、釀造方式），目前（當前條件下，如何享受）和未來（它會如何發展，可以保留多久）。

如通用術語，共同的環境中，常見的工具等。

## 投影片 5

A. Environments / 環境
Ideally, the room temperature is at 18 to 22ºC with 60 to 70% of humidity in a quiet room with no odor, good ventilation, good lighting, light coloured wall and a table with a white table cloth or mat.

理想情況下，室內溫度在18在安靜的房間與無異味，透氣性好，採光好，淺色的牆壁和一台用白色桌布或墊子與濕度60％到70％至22℃。

## 投影片 6

B. Glass / 杯子
For a wine tasting with a technical point of view, it is preferable to use the standard tasting glass of ISO (International Standard Organization).
Otherwise, 6 to 8 oz tulip shaped glasses without cutting are generally used.

以品酒的技術角度來看，優選的是使用ISO標準的杯子（國際標準組織）。否則，採用一般都6至8盎司鬱金香形杯子。

## 投影片 16

Clarity　清晰度
Check if the wine is healthy. Wine loses clarity and presents cloudiness if any kind of problem takes place during bottling, aging and transportation. Wines are generally clear but some of “non-filtered”, “non-collage” wines and highly tannic wines look cloudy even when they are healthy.

　　檢查葡萄酒是否良好，如果在裝瓶和運輸過程中發生任何問題，葡萄酒就會失去清晰度並呈現混濁。

　　葡萄酒通常是很清晰，但一些“無過濾”或“混合的”葡萄酒和高單寧性葡萄酒即使在健康的情況下也會看起來有些渾濁。
16

## 投影片 17

Brilliance　亮度
Shine and gloss of wine is observed by reflection of light. This also confirms healthiness of wine.
Brilliance is closely related with acidity. Brilliance gets higher when acidity is higher as it stabilizes colour components in wine. A wine with high acidity always presents remarkable brilliance.

通過反射光觀察酒的亮度和光澤。這也證實了葡萄酒的良好與否。亮度與酸度有密切的相關。當酸度較高時，亮度會變得更亮，因為它可以穩定葡萄酒的顏色成分，而高酸度的葡萄酒總是呈現出非凡的光彩。
17

## 投影片 18

Colour　顏色
By observing colour, total characteristics of the wine can be seen. As colour of wine changes by oxidation, it is a factor to judge maturation level and age of wine. To observe the colour of a wine, lean the glass as the oval surface of the wine well presents changing colours. In general, wines of cool climate regions present bluish pale colours.

通過觀察顏色，可以看到葡萄酒的總體特徵。 由於葡萄酒的顏色因氧化而變化，所以它是判斷葡萄酒成熟程度和年分的一個因素。 要觀察葡萄酒的顏色要將酒杯呈45度角斜靠在白色燈光或布上，依據酒杯的橢圓形面所呈現不同變化的顏色來觀察。 一般來說，涼爽氣候地區的白葡萄酒呈淡青色。
18

## 投影片 19

Colour　顏色
Wines of warmer climate regions present deep and dark red colours without bluish nuances. Color of white wine changes from gold to amber by aging. Rose wine changes into deeper orange colour. Red wine changes from purplish red to orange then to a brick colour.

溫暖的氣候地區的紅葡萄酒呈深沉而深紅色。 老化的白葡萄酒顏色由金黃色變為琥珀色。 紅葡萄酒從紫紅色變為橙色，然後變成紅磚色。
19

## 投影片 20

Intensity　強度
This is the most important factor in appearance as intensity shows the level of grape maturity and intensity of wine flavours. In general, wines of warmer climate regions have a darker colour and wines from cool climate regions have a paler colour.

強度：這是外觀上最重要的觀察，因為強度顯示了葡萄酒的風味與葡萄的成熟度。 一般來說，溫暖氣候地區的葡萄酒顏色較深，來自涼爽氣候地區的葡萄酒顏色較淡。
20

## 投影片 21

Viscosity　粘度
Viscosity also indicates alcohol level and amount of glycerol. Level of viscosity is judged by the condition of “Tears” (legs) flowing down along interior of glass. In general, viscosity gets higher as the alcohol level is higher and at the same time glycerol produced by alcohol fermentation increases.

Sweet wines with extremely high level of residual sugar such as noble rot wine are also high is viscosity.

粘度也表示酒精的含量和甘油量。 粘度的觀察是通過轉動玻璃杯後, 杯身流下的“淚滴”來判斷。 一般來說，酒精濃度越高，粘度越高，同時酒精發酵產生的甘油也越多。
而具有高殘糖含量的甜葡萄酒如貴腐酒也是高粘度。
21

## 投影片 22

Fizziness　氣泡
Young still wines sometime present fizziness as carbon dioxide gas has not completely escaped from the wines. Some wines are produced by the method which intentionally retains the gas in wine.

年輕的葡萄酒有時會出現氣泡，是由於發酵時產生的二氧化碳氣體還沒有完全從葡萄酒中消失。而有些葡萄酒是故意保留微量的氣體所產生的。
22

## 投影片 23

<Nose> / 嗅覺
Primary Aromas, Secondary Aromas, Bouquet
主要香氣(果香)，進階香氣(發酵香)，醇香(陳釀香)
More information is given by analyzing aromas to identify characters of grape varieties, vinification methods, maturity, aging potential regional characters etc.

通過分析香氣來鑑定葡萄品種的特徵，釀造方法，成熟度，儲存狀況及地區性特徵等，可以獲得更多的信息。
23

## 投影片 24

Aromas are classified into three categories as follows.
香氣分為以下三類
Primary Aromas (Aromes Primaires)
 主要香氣(果香)

Aromas orginated in material grapes such as aromas of “fruits” “flowers” “spices” “mineral”.

初級香氣有
“水果”“花朵”“香料” “礦物”等香氣
24

## 投影片 25

Secondary Aromas (Aromes Secondaires)
進階香氣(發酵香)
Aromas originated in fermentation process (alcoholic fermentation, malo-lactic
fermentation etc.) Wines have aromas of “candy” and “ginjyo-sake” in case of low
temperature fermentation. “Banana” aroma is from carbonic maceration. Bright and
smooth aromas of “almond jelly” and “custard cream” are from malo-lactic fermentation.

進階香氣源自於發酵過程（酒精發酵，乳酸發酵等）。如果酒在低溫發酵的情況下，葡萄酒會
有“糖果”“日本酒Sake”的發酵香氣。“香蕉味”的香氣來自二氧化碳浸泡法的釀造方式。
清爽和“杏仁味”和“奶油吐司味”的平滑香氣則是來自乳酸發酵。
25

## 投影片 26

Bouquet/Tertiary Aromas (Bouquet/Aromas Territories): 醇香(陳釀香) / 第三級香味
    Bouquet stands for aromas originated in maturation in barrel or bottle.  These aromas
include “vanilla”, “roast” and “spices” originated in wooden barrel and also complicated aromas generated by oxidative maturation of the primary aromas and secondary aromas. These aromas are further developed to be wonderful bouquet by integration during the bottle aging process.

醇香表示這葡萄酒於橡木桶或瓶中成熟的芳香。 這些香味包括起源自於木桶的“香草”，
“焦香”和“香料”，以及由主要香味和次級香味的氧化成熟所產生的複雜香味。而這些香氣被進一步在瓶子陳釀過程中通過整合而發展成為美妙的醇香。
26

## 投影片 27

<Taste>　口感
Attack, Acidity, Sweetness, Tannic Taste (Astringency), Bitterness, Flavors, Alcohol, Body, Intensity , Balance, Mineral, Finesse, Sharpness, Length
衝擊性：酸度、甜度、單寧味（澀味） 、苦味、口味、酒精、酒體強烈度：平衡、礦物質、細膩，
刺激性：後醞
By employing modern vinification techniques, it is possible to produce wines with fairly rich aromas. The important factor to judge the basic value of a wine is the taste. Production region, aging potential and value of the wine can be judged by analyzing the taste. Senses of smell and touch as well as sense of taste should be fully activated to analyze the taste.
通過採用現代釀酒技術，可以生產出具有相當豐富香氣的葡萄酒。判斷葡萄酒基本價值的重要因素是品嚐。 通過分析口感可以判斷出產區，葡萄酒的陳年潛力和價值。應該充分利用感官包括：嗅覺、口感以及味覺來分析整體的味道。
27

## 投影片 28

Attack衝擊性
The first impression of wine when it enters the mouth and it indicates strength of the wine.

葡萄酒入口時的第一印象，它表明葡萄酒的強度。

## 投影片 29

Acidity酸度
This is an important factor of wine to form its character. It indicates the characters of grape variety, climate of the production region, topography (altitudes etc.) and youthfulness.

這是葡萄酒形成其特性的重要因素。 它表明葡萄品種，生產地區的溫度氣候，地形（海拔等）和生長期的特點。

## 投影片 30

Sweetness
(Level of sweetness and dryness)
甜度(甜味程度及乾)
Residual sugar, fruitiness of fully ripen grapes, alcohol and glycerol are sensed as sweetness.

殘糖含量，成熟的果味，酒精和甘油被感覺為甜味。
30

## 投影片 31

Tannic Taste (Astringency)
單寧味（澀味）
Tannins in wine are felt as tannic taste and astringency on the palate. It is an important component in red wines with longevity. In general, intensity of colour is in proportion to astringency of wine.

葡萄酒中的丹寧在口感上感覺為單寧味和澀味。 這是紅酒可以長期儲放的重要因素。
一般來說，顏色的強度與葡萄酒的澀感成正比。
31

## 投影片 32

Bitterness苦味
Bitterness in all wines of white, rose and red indicates that the material grapes were grown under abundant sunshine (a high maturity level)

白葡萄酒，玫瑰紅葡萄酒和紅葡萄酒中的苦味表示，這葡萄生長在充足的陽光下（高熟度 / 高酒精）
32

## 投影片 33

Flavours　口味
Flavours mean aromas and tastes of wine including those originated in grape varieties and also in fermentation and maturation in processes.

風味意味著葡萄酒的香味和口味，包括起源於葡萄品種的葡萄酒以及過程中的發酵和成熟。

## 投影片 34

Alcohol　酒精
Like acidity, alcohol is an important component of wine. It gives sweetness, weight, length and structure to wine.

像酸度一樣，酒精是葡萄酒的重要組成部分。
它賦予了酒的甜味，酒體，後醞和結構。

## 投影片 35

酒體用來表示葡萄酒的豐富度和厚度。

## 投影片 36

Intensity　強烈度

強烈度是指葡萄酒各式成分的濃度，深度和厚度。

## 投影片 37

葡萄酒各式成分的平衡和諧度。

精緻，優雅和優雅的葡萄酒，表明了葡萄酒是如何精確的被製作。
Sharpness刺激性

刺激性的強弱是基於酸度的強度。

## 投影片 39

Aftertaste  /  Length後醞
Aftertaste means flavours which remain after swallowing a wine. Persistence and strength of the flavours indicate maturity and intensity of wine.

回味意味著在吞下葡萄酒後所留下的味道。
後醞的持久性和強度表明葡萄酒的成熟度和強度

綜合以上因素可以判斷葡萄酒的成熟度和陳年潛力。
